Why

why data centers

AI runs on data centers, and they’re going up fast. They draw power and water from the same grids and aquifers as the towns around them, often under nondisclosure agreements and tax breaks residents hear about later. Meanwhile a few companies buy into and partner with each other, spend heavily on lobbying, and win public contracts.

People feel those decisions in their utility bills, water, jobs and privacy, but the news comes in pieces, scattered across utility filings, merger paperwork and local papers. Tracewire is the second tracker built with Paper Trail, and it keeps those pieces together.

Speeds

two speeds

Tracewire has two speeds and keeps them apart. The Wire pulls raw headlines from 52 public feeds (the FTC, Justice Department, SEC filings, federal courts, energy regulators, newsrooms) every few minutes, marked unverified. The Feed is the checked record. Every hour a scheduled Claude run reads each item at its source, labels it confirmed, reported or disputed, and tags the companies, agencies and places involved.

  • Saved copies. Every source link is saved to the Internet Archive, so the record survives broken links.
  • A status page. What each check read, which sources were healthy, and what couldn’t be fetched.
  • Never in its own voice. It doesn’t call anything corruption; it says what was filed, charged or reported, and by whom.
  • Nothing deleted. Corrections in place, older posts archived, every change on GitHub.
Explore

follow a company or a deal

There’s more than the feed. Tracewire tracks over a hundred companies, agencies, utilities, courts and officials, keeps a ledger of deals, fines, contracts and lobbying, maps data center projects by state, and draws who is buying, investing in and partnering with whom.

Dives

deep dives

Beyond the feed, Tracewire has deep dives: one question at a time, dated, with every assumption checked and every source linked. I build them with Claude and check them before they go up.
