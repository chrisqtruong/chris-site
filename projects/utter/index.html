Utter

Dictation for iPhone, built for long thoughts. Strong speech models turn your voice into text on the phone, and nothing ever leaves it.

see it on github
What

talk for as long as you want, read it back as notes

Tap the dot and talk, for a few seconds or half an hour, even with the screen locked. When you stop, the text is ready and copied. Pauses become paragraphs, ums and uhs disappear, and longer notes get a short title.

Built-in dictation is made for quick replies. Utter is for the longer stuff: monologues, journaling, ideas on a walk, notes you want to keep.

Trust

it tells you how sure it is

Every note gets a match score from 0 to 100, from how sure the speech model was about each word. Words it wasn’t sure about get a dotted underline, so you know where to look before you send anything.

A one-minute voice check measures how well each model hears your voice, teaches it the names you use, and tunes the score so 85 means about 85% of your words came out right. I wrote up how the score works, including where it fails.

Principles

four rules it’s built on

  1. On the phone. No account, no servers. Recordings are thrown away once they’re text.
  2. Pick your model. Parakeet or Whisper, with honest pros and cons. Download only what you use.
  3. One button. Tap to start, tap to stop. Everything else stays out of the way.
  4. Charming, not slick. 37 themes and the same hand-drawn marks as this site.
