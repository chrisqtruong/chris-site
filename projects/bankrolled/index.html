Bankrolled

A sourced record of the money behind American gambling: who pays whom, who loses, and who writes the rules.

read it github
Why

why gambling

A few years ago sports betting was illegal in most states. Now it’s an app on everyone’s phone. Bettors lost $17 billion to legal sportsbooks in 2025, nearly four times what they lost in 2021. Bankrolled keeps the political money, the deals, the lawsuits and the research on harm in one place.

Losses

five years of losing

Since you opened this page, bettors have lost about $0 to legal sportsbooks.

What sportsbooks kept each year, which is what bettors lost, from the American Gaming Association. The counter runs at the 2025 pace, about $538 a second.

Rules

the rules

  • Every item is labeled. Confirmed, reported or disputed.
  • Charged isn’t convicted. Lawsuits are allegations.
  • Nothing deleted. Corrections are made in place, with the date.

How

how it works

Every hour, a scheduled Claude task checks the primary records first (FEC filings, lobbying disclosures, court dockets, regulators), then the news. It reads each item at its source before it labels it, and writes a dated report even when nothing met the bar.

  1. primary recordsFEC, lobbying, courts
  2. the newswire, national, trade
  3. read at the sourceduplicates skipped
  4. label itconfirmed, reported, disputed
  5. publishand a dated report

So far that's 48 posts (43 confirmed, 4 reported, 1 disputed) and 68 rows in the ledger, which only takes confirmed items. The checks are paused for now; everything published stays up.
