Purrmodoro

A cozy little focus timer for iPhone, starring a brown cat who studies with you. Made as a surprise gift for my wife.

01Why

a timer that doesn't feel like homework

My wife works across lots of different tasks. She wanted a simple rhythm (focus, break, focus) and a gentle way to see how much focused time she'd put in. The apps out there were either bland or overloaded.

Add a line of your own: what made you decide to build it yourself, and how she reacted when she first opened it.

02Principles

five rules i wrote before anything else

  1. Open, tap, focusing. It should never need setup to use.
  2. Task-agnostic. No categories, labels or to-dos.
  3. She's in control. Nothing starts without a tap.
  4. Lightweight. No third-party libraries, no accounts, no servers.
  5. Delight in small doses. Smooth animations and haptics, never in the way.
03Character

her room follows the real clock

The cat sits at her desk inside the timer ring. By day she wears a white coat and has an iced coffee; after sunset it's pajamas, round glasses, a warm lamp and tea. The window shows the actual time of day, from dawn colors to stars. Everything is drawn in code, no image files.

When she's not focusing, you can pet her. Ears flick, a nose boop gets >_<, a paw tap gets a wave, and one in ten belly rubs gets a rare purr.

04Scope

what i left out, on purpose

Task lists, categories, accounts, sync, ambient music and an Apple Watch app all stayed out of version one. A few ideas are tabled for later:

  1. Ring even on silent, with Apple's new alarm tools.
  2. Collectibles: treats, outfits and rare cats earned through focus time. Her full history is saved, so they can be awarded retroactively.
  3. Real weather in the cat's window.
