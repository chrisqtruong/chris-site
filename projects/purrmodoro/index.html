Purrmodoro

A cozy little focus timer for iPhone, starring a brown kitty who studies with you. Made as a surprise gift for my wife.

01Why

a timer that doesn't feel like homework

My wife works across lots of different tasks. She wanted a simple rhythm (focus, break, focus) and a gentle way to see how much focused time she'd put in. The apps out there were either bland or overloaded.

Add a line of your own: what made you decide to build it yourself, and how she reacted when she first opened it.

02Principles

five rules i wrote before anything else

  1. Open, tap, focusing. It should never need setup to use.
  2. Task-agnostic. No categories, labels or to-dos.
  3. She's in control. Nothing starts without a tap.
  4. Lightweight. No third-party libraries, no accounts, no servers.
  5. Delight in small doses. Smooth animations and haptics, never in the way.

04Character

her room follows the real clock

The kitty sits at her desk inside the timer ring. By day she wears a white coat and has an iced coffee; after sunset it's pajamas, round glasses, a warm lamp and tea. The window shows the actual time of day, from dawn colors to stars. Everything is drawn in code, no image files.

When she's not focusing, you can pet her. Ears flick, a nose boop gets >_<, a paw tap gets a wave, and one in ten belly rubs gets a rare purr.

05Design

details you feel more than see

wind the clock

Drag around the ring like a kitchen timer: halfway is 30 minutes, a full turn is 60. A haptic tick each minute, a soft click every five, and the kitty's eyes follow your finger.

two kinds of focus

“Focus time” counts every minute. “Deep focus” only counts sessions finished start to end with no pauses, the honest heads-down number.

words with a wink

Thirty little lines rotate under the timer, shuffled so none repeat until all have shown. Breaks get their own.

Paws on the keyboardWhiskers forwardPawsitively focusedSlow blink, like a catDoing nothing is allowed

sounds made from scratch

Marimba, music box and singing bowl alarms, plus pops and purrs, all synthesized for this app. Each alarm has a lower version for when a break ends.

07Scope

what i left out, on purpose

Task lists, categories, accounts, sync, ambient music and an Apple Watch app all stayed out of version one. A few ideas are tabled for later:

  1. Ring even on silent, with Apple's new alarm tools.
  2. Collectibles: treats, outfits and rare kitties earned through focus time. Her full history is saved, so they can be awarded retroactively.
  3. Real weather in the kitty's window.
