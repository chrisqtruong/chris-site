Your story goes here. A few prompts: When did you first wish this existed? Who were you trying to talk to (family, in-laws, strangers while traveling)? What did Google Translate in a browser tab get wrong for you?

02Design

small decisions that make it feel calm

words settle, they don't pop

New words show grey until the engine is sure, so the text never jumps around while you type.

Construyo las cosas q

trust, not just translation

The translation comes back into your language with a score for how much of your meaning survived. It compares meaning, not exact words.

↩ Hello, I'm Chris.98% match

37 themes, readable for everyone

Color is personal, so there are lots of themes. Scores can switch to a colorblind-safe palette with ✓ ! ✕ symbols, so meaning never depends on color alone.

private by default

No account, no server, no analytics. Voice, screen snips and the meaning check run on your own computer.

03Testing

testing my own feature, honestly

A score is only useful if you can trust it, so I tested the meaning check against 1,320 translations in 11 languages, including ones with deliberate mistakes.

93%of good translations correctly shown as “meaning kept”
100%of changed numbers caught
29%of other meaning errors caught, like a flipped “not”

That last number isn't good enough yet, and the ↩ line usually shows the mistake even when the score misses it. So fixing it went to the top of the roadmap.
